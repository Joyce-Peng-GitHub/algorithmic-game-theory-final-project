## Superpower of GPT-5.6

The cutting-edge large language model, GPT-5.6-Sol, **independently** noticed the defect of the paper with its sharp insight, and even successfully came up with a corrective approach. [The session file](./rollout-2026-08-10T23-23-00-019fec45-1dcb-7c92-aa6c-eb8ddfd703d7.jsonl), which verbosely shows my prompts as well as the model's responses and tool calls, can prove that GPT really did it without either searching the internet or reading Prof. Liu's paper. I'm willing to offer other supporting materials including our `git` commits and chat history.

My original intention was to let GPT explain the paper to me and assist me with our English report. Nevertheless, when I asked it to check if there were errors, it pointed out the "fake" query efficiency, which led me to further explore the problem with it. I must admit that the main  credit should be attributed to GPT, but if I did not ask it to check my report or decide to dive into the problem for further investigation, it would not propose the correction on its own. Moreover, we humans have carefully reviewed its method and verified its correctness --- we take responsibility for our report.

LLMs have recently shown exceptional excellence in frontier math, picking low-hanging fruits everywhere. It may be an opportunity worthy of grasping.
