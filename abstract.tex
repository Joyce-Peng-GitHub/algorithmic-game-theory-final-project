\begin{abstract}
This report presents expository and corrective reading notes on the polynomial-time algorithm of Deligkas, Fasoulakis, and Markakis that computes a \((1/2+\delta)\)-WSNE in bimatrix games, improving the previous \(0.6528\) guarantee. We reconstruct the deductions that the source omits or compresses, making explicit how zero-sum equilibria, linear feasibility, and constant-support enumeration establish correctness in every branch. We also audit the randomized payoff-query variant: we correct the success probability to \(p_n^2(1-\eta-\zeta)\), accounting for sampling and estimation failures, and repair a query-accounting gap in the branch tests via statistical estimates with explicit slack, all while preserving the asymptotic query and running-time bounds and the approximation guarantees.
\end{abstract}
