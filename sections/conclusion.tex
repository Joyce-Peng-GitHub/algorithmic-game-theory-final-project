\section{Conclusion}

The paper's main significance is both quantitative and structural: it makes the WSNE threshold $1/2+\delta$ attainable in polynomial time while replacing increasingly elaborate approximation schemes with a transparent combination of zero-sum optimization, linear feasibility, and constant-support enumeration. The query-efficient extension shows that this structure survives under partial payoff access. The probability-accounting corrections above leave both approximation guarantees and asymptotic complexities intact, while making explicit the randomized assumptions needed for Algorithm~2. Replacing the exact branch tests with statistical estimates and explicit slack likewise preserves both the asymptotic query bound and the approximation guarantee of the query-efficient version, at the price of a reduced success probability.
